# Quality gates

Run the relevant gates before execution, reporting, archival, or publication.

## Data integrity

- Keep planned budget and revenue separate from actual cost and revenue.
- Trace every `ACTUAL` result to an approved source.
- Show `SIMULATED` and `TO_VALIDATE` labels beside demonstration or incomplete figures.
- Reconcile registration, check-in, satisfaction, UGC, income, cost, cases, and supplier data before using them in an overall report.
- Calculate actual ROI as `(actual revenue - actual cost) / actual cost`; use `N/A` when actual cost is zero.

## Activity before

- Assign an owner and due date for critical people, material, user, budget, and rundown items.
- Confirm participant fee rules, refund rules, invoicing, and payment collection before recruitment.
- Confirm venue, supplier, spend, filming, participant-data, accessibility, and publication approvals.
- Keep an editable source record after importing or generating a plan.

## Activity during

- Verify core contact details and backups before the event starts.
- Keep manual add and edit paths available for tasks, contacts, incidents, and rundown changes.
- Record incident time, severity, owner, action, and follow-up.
- Include crowd, medical, fire, weather, device, network, supplier, accessibility, and privacy contingencies when applicable.

## Activity after

- Confirm attendance denominator and survey denominator.
- Record actual revenue and actual cost, including unplanned on-site adjustments.
- Track every supplier through its own applicable payment nodes and confirm receipt or closure.
- Track complaints and cases through resolution and proof of closure.
- Separate UGC by channel and content type; record authorization before reuse.
- Record suggestion, retrospective, and reusable lessons for the next event.
- Archive only when required data, supplier nodes, feedback, and actual ROI have been reviewed.
- Keep cancelled-event records separate from completed-event archives.

## Privacy and publication

- Remove API keys, phone numbers, personal email, attendee lists, payment details, raw complaints, confidential contracts, and identifying faces from public artifacts.
- Use fictional or redacted contacts and suppliers in examples.
- State when figures are simulated or portfolio-only.
- Require human approval before external outreach, spend, publication, refund, or safety-critical action.
